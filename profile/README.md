## Apps

| App | What it does | Get it |
|---|---|---|
| [KashCal](https://kashcal.onekash.org) | All your calendars in one Android app: iCloud and other CalDAV servers, plus your phone's Google and Outlook calendars | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.onekash.kashcal) · [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.onekash.kashcal/) · [Source](https://github.com/KashCal/KashCal) |
| [KashCake](https://kashcake.onekash.org) | A reminder before every birthday and anniversary in your contacts | [Google Play](https://kashcake.onekash.org/beta/) · [Source](https://github.com/OneKashLabs/KashCake) |
| [KashPass](https://kashpass.onekash.org) | Your KeePass vault on your phone | [Google Play](https://kashpass.onekash.org/beta/) · [Source](https://github.com/OneKashLabs/KashPass) |
| [iCloud Calendar MCP](https://mcp.onekash.org) | Connect AI agents to your iCloud Calendar | [Install](https://mcp.onekash.org) · [Source](https://github.com/icloud-calendar-mcp/icloud-calendar-mcp) |

KashCake and KashPass have no internet access.
